# Recursion 0.3.0-beta.1

Recursion's first beta brings a week of improvements to how it selects scene cards, checks their guidance, and revises a finished reply.

**Your important cards stay in the hand.** Priority cards are required in Auto, including authored cards. Fused and Segmented use the same card plan, and incomplete required preparation stops before narration. New variety and cooldown controls give optional Auto selections room to change between turns.

**Refinement gives important cards a second look.** Mark a card for Refinement to require a scene-grounded review before narration. Recursion can accept it unchanged or revise and verify it, and the Viewer shows the assessment. Optional Realism analysis encourages characters to question extraordinary claims, consider familiar explanations, and seek evidence appropriate to the setting.

**Post-process gets a dedicated writer and review workflow.** Use the current SillyTavern model or a separate Connection Profile to write revisions. Choose Polish or Revise, supply a deck style, and inspect or edit a candidate before applying it as a swipe or replacement.

**Recovery is clearer and more automatic.** Fused runs without a certification gate, retains valid card results during repair, and reports recovered progress accurately. Temporary rate limits wait and retry with a shared profile cooldown. Successful internal recovery stays quiet, while Stop, Resume, and Retry preserve the relevant work and waiting state.

The update also preserves complete selected-message and card text, keeps Viewer scrolling stable, removes duplicate card details, and makes diagnostics a JSON download on HTTP/LAN setups.

Update Recursion in SillyTavern, reload, and review your deck states and provider settings. Refinement adds review work, so start with the cards that matter most. This remains a beta: model output quality and provider limits still vary.

Read the [full release notes](0.3.0-beta.1.md) or the [operator manual](../user/RECURSION_OPERATOR_MANUAL.md) for settings, recovery behavior, and verification details.
